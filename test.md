The idempotency key is the UETR generated upstream by the Payment Network Gateway, and the same value is passed to FIS, so a retried payment cannot be processed twice. Access is OAuth 2.0 client-credentials over CodeConnect. The call returns 202 on acceptance, or one of the synchronous error codes above. No pain.002 or MQ path is used in Phase 1. Account-number encryption is not part of this contract: the payload carries full account numbers, protected in transit by TLS over CodeConnect only. Sending them without payload-level encryption is covered by a UTEP. Whether FIS can offer payload-level encryption (CodeConnect lists a payload-encryption key) is pending with FIS.
